Frequently asked
questions

How is OREHA different from an energy gel?

Made in Japan, OREHA combines a Palatinose-based carbohydrate blend with a smooth jelly texture—less sticky on the run and easier on the stomach.

How many carbohydrates are in each pouch?

Peach contains 21.3 g, Grape 22.7 g and Honey Lemon 26.6 g of carbohydrates per 45 g pouch.

When should I take OREHA?

Before or during endurance exercise. Practise with OREHA in training to find the timing and amount that work for you.

What’s inside OREHA?

Palatinose, fructose and dextrin provide carbohydrates. MCT oil adds energy from fat, magnesium supports normal muscle function, and agar creates the jelly texture.

Is OREHA vegan?

Honey Lemon contains honey and is not vegan. See each flavour’s ingredient list for dietary and allergen information.

Is OREHA tested for banned substances?

OREHA Gel Energy is Informed Choice certified. View the certification directory for product details and tested-batch records.
